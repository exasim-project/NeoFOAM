\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Spalart-\/\+Allmaras DDES turbulence model. 

Registered under the key "{}\+Spalart\+Allmaras\+DDES"{} in the \doxylink{classNeoFOAM_1_1TurbulenceModel}{Turbulence\+Model} factory.

Two distinct wall-\/distance fields are required\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item wall\+Dist \+: cell-\/centred distances (Foam\+::wall\+Dist\+::y()), for DDES shielding
\item near\+Wall\+Dist \+: boundary-\/face distances (turbulence\+Model\+::y()\mbox{[}patchi\mbox{]}), for wall BCs
\end{DoxyItemize}

The model owns nu\+Tilda and nut internally. Call \doxylink{classNeoFOAM_1_1SpalartAllmarasDDES_ac43050c3c177a80bad14cf5a56284c6c}{initialize()} with disk-\/read \doxylink{namespaceNeoN}{NeoN} fields before the first \doxylink{classNeoFOAM_1_1SpalartAllmarasDDES_a3c0ce97c52d3cc20099747f4728acda0}{validate()} to seed non-\/zero initial conditions. The backward-\/compatible three-\/argument validate(\+U, nu\+Tilda, nut) and correct(\+U, phi, nu\+Tilda, nut, rt) overloads are retained for callers that manage those fields externally (e.\+g. neo\+Piso\+Foam).
