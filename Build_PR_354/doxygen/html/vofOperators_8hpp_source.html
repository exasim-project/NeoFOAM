27/* @brief fvc::flux(phi, alpha, "Gauss vanLeer"): the vanLeer-limited convective

29 *
30 * @detail alpha_f uses the limited interpolation weight
31 * w_f = limiter*wCD + (1 - limiter)*pos0(phi_f)
32 * wCD = SfdNei/(SfdOwn + SfdNei) (linear/CD weight)
