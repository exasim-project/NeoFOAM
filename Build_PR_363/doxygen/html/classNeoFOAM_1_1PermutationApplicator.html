Detailed Description

Applies cell permutations to mesh and cell-associated data.

PermutationApplicator applies a valid cell permutation to NeoN mesh data while preserving the consistency of cell-associated data and references.

A cell permutation has two distinct effects:

  • Cell-associated data, such as cell fields and cell geometry, is reordered according to the permutation.
  • Cell references stored in mesh connectivity, such as face owner and neighbor indices, are remapped according to the permutation.

The aplicator does not:

  • compute permutations
  • modify permutation objects
  • select ordering algorithms
  • perform implicit host/device data transfers

Applies a cell permutation to a mesh.

Cell-associated mesh data is reordered according to permutation, while cell indices stored in mesh connectivity are remapped to their corresponding new indices.

In particular, cell-associated data is reordered by position, whereas face owner and neighbour indices are remapped by value.

Parameters
meshMesh whose cell-related data is to be reordered.
permutationPermutation defining the mapping from old cell indices to new cell indices.
Precondition
permutation is a valid permutation over the cells of mesh.
Postcondition
Cell-associated mesh data and cell references are consistent with the new cell ordering.
