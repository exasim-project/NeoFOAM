\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Applies cell permutations to mesh and cell-\/associated data. 

\doxylink{classNeoFOAM_1_1PermutationApplicator}{Permutation\+Applicator} applies a valid cell permutation to \doxylink{namespaceNeoN}{NeoN} mesh data while preserving the consistency of cell-\/associated data and references.

A cell permutation has two distinct effects\+:


\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Cell-\/associated data, such as cell fields and cell geometry, is reordered according to the permutation.
\item Cell references stored in mesh connectivity, such as face owner and neighbor indices, are remapped according to the permutation.
\end{DoxyItemize}

The aplicator does not\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item compute permutations
\item modify permutation objects
\item select ordering algorithms
\item perform implicit host/device data transfers 
\end{DoxyItemize}

Applies a cell permutation to a mesh. 

Cell-\/associated mesh data is reordered according to {\ttfamily permutation}, while cell indices stored in mesh connectivity are remapped to their corresponding new indices.

In particular, cell-\/associated data is reordered by position, whereas face owner and neighbour indices are remapped by value.
