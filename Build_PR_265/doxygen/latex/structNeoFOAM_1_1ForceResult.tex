\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Accumulated force and moment vectors from a \doxylink{classNeoFOAM_1_1Forces}{Forces} execution. 

Both pressure and viscous force/moment slots are populated by execute()\+: the pressure contribution from the boundary pressure field and the viscous contribution from the wall shear stress (grad\+U-\/based, including turbulent nut when present).
