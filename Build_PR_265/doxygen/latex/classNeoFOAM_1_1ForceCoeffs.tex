\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Normalised aerodynamic force coefficients derived from GPU force integrals. 

Extends \doxylink{classNeoFOAM_1_1Forces}{Forces} by dividing the raw pressure forces and moments by the dynamic pressure reference quantities to produce dimensionless coefficients.
