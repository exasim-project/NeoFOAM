● Full analysis: cylinder2D divergence — root cause found and fixed

  The bug

  Laminar::nut_ is allocated but never initialized, in
  src/turbulenceModels/laminar.cpp. The VolumeField(exec, name, mesh, BCs)
  constructor only allocates from the Umpire pool, which does not zero memory —
  so nut_ held garbage, even though the header documents "nut is identically
  zero".

  neoSimpleFoam's momentum equation has an explicit term exp::viscousStress(nu,
  nut, gradU) that computes nuEff = nu + nut per cell. The garbage nut poisoned
  the momentum source → HbyA/U blew to ~60 at the very first step → the SIMPLE
  outer loop amplified it geometrically to nan by ~step 13.

  How I localized it

  1. Trajectory triage: continuity sum local was huge from step 1 (0.84) and
  grew ~10×/step regardless of scheme/relaxation — across all your A–G/INV
  variants the trajectories were near-identical. Heavy relaxation only delayed
  blow-up → a consistency/code bug, not under-resolution. checkMesh confirmed
  the mesh is well-conditioned (max non-orth 44°), so mesh wasn't it.
  2. Instrumented neoSimpleFoam to print div(phiHbyA), maxRAU, maxHbyA, maxU,
  max|p| per step. Smoking gun: at step 1, with p still 0, the momentum
  predictor produced maxU=62 and maxHbyA=60 — the pressure loop was faithfully
  chasing an already-broken velocity. → bug is in momentum assembly/HbyA, not
  pressure coupling.
  3. Bisected the new (steady-only) terms. neoIcoFoam (which works) has none of:
  bounded div, viscousStress, turbulence, relaxation. Disabling viscousStress
  made the case stable → the term, or its inputs, was the culprit.
  4. The sign/magnitude of viscousStress itself was correct vs OpenFOAM's
  divDevReff; tracing its inputs showed nut_ is read uninitialized.
