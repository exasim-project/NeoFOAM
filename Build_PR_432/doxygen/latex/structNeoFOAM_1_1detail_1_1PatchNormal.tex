\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Mean unit normal of an Open\+FOAM patch and how far its faces deviate from it. 

\doxylink{namespaceNeoN}{NeoN}\textquotesingle{}s fixed\+Value carries a single value for the whole patch, so a boundary condition defined per face along the surface normal has to collapse to one normal. {\ttfamily deviation} is the largest \texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|}n\+\_\+f -\/ mean\+Normal\texorpdfstring{$\vert$}{|} over the patch faces\+: 0 on a planar patch (where the collapse is exact), O(1) on a strongly curved one.
