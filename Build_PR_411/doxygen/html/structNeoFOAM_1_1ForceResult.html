Detailed Description

Accumulated force and moment vectors from a Forces execution.

Both pressure and viscous force/moment slots are populated by execute(): the pressure contribution from the boundary pressure field and the viscous contribution from the wall shear stress (gradU-based, including turbulent nut when present).
